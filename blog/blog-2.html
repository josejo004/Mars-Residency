Top 5 Hidden Places in Kanyakumari You Must Visit

Discover peaceful beaches, historic places, beautiful landscapes and hidden gems beyond the popular tourist attractions.

KANYAKUMARI HIDDEN PLACES

Updated: August 2026

Kanyakumari is famous for its sunrise, sunset, Vivekananda Rock Memorial and Thiruvalluvar Statue. However, there are many beautiful places around the district that visitors often miss.

If you want to explore beyond the usual tourist attractions, these places offer beaches, history, nature and peaceful landscapes.

1. Vattakottai Fort

Vattakottai Fort is a historic seaside fort surrounded by beautiful coastal scenery. It is a great place to explore history while enjoying views of the sea and surrounding landscape.

Visitors can walk around the fort, enjoy the coastal views and spend time near the nearby beach.

📍 Get Directions

2. Chothavilai Beach

Chothavilai Beach is a peaceful and scenic beach located away from the busy tourist areas of Kanyakumari.

The long sandy coastline makes it a relaxing destination for visitors who want to spend time near the sea.

📍 Get Directions

3. Muttom Beach

Muttom Beach is known for its dramatic rocky coastline and beautiful sea views. The waves hitting the large rocks create a unique and memorable atmosphere.

It is a good destination for visitors who enjoy photography, coastal scenery and quieter surroundings.

📍 Get Directions

4. Mathur Hanging Trough

Mathur Hanging Trough is an impressive elevated aqueduct surrounded by green countryside and beautiful valley views.

The area offers a very different experience from the beaches of Kanyakumari and is a great place for travellers interested in nature and engineering landmarks.

📍 Get Directions

5. Keeriparai and Vattaparai Falls

The Keeriparai area offers a more natural and peaceful side of Kanyakumari district. Forest scenery, streams and waterfalls make it a good destination for nature lovers.

Vattaparai Falls is located in the Keeriparai reserve forest area and is surrounded by natural greenery.

📍 Get Directions

Travel Tips for Exploring Hidden Places in Kanyakumari

  • Start your journey early in the morning.
  • Carry drinking water when travelling to remote areas.
  • Check weather conditions before visiting waterfalls.
  • Wear comfortable footwear for walking.
  • Use Google Maps to plan your route.
  • Respect local communities and natural surroundings.
