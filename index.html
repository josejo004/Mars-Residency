Who We Are

A homely stay, built for the way Kanya Kumari moves

Mars Residency sits minutes from Kanya Kumari's temples, markets and railway station — rooms kept genuinely clean, staff who remember your name by day two, and a reception that never really closes.

Our Hospitality

Every guest is greeted like a returning one. From early check-ins for overnight trains to late check-outs after a long temple day, our team bends the schedule, not the guest.

Our Mission

To offer dependable, spotless, well-priced rooms in the heart of Kanya Kumari — without the impersonal feel of a big chain hotel.

Why Choose Us

Central location, honest pricing, and a team that treats housekeeping and hospitality as the same job — done well, every single day.

Around Kanyakumari

Places worth stepping out for

Explore the iconic sights of Kanyakumari, all within easy reach of Mars Residency.

Vivekananda Rock Memorial

Approx. 5 km • 15–20 min drive

One of Kanyakumari's most iconic landmarks, located on a rocky island surrounded by the sea.

Get Directions →

Thiruvalluvar Statue & Glass Bridge

Approx. 5 km • 15–20 min drive

See the magnificent 133-foot Thiruvalluvar Statue and the spectacular Glass Bridge across the sea.

Get Directions →

Kumari Amman Temple

Approx. 4.5 km • 15 min drive

A famous seaside temple dedicated to Goddess Kanyakumari, located at the southern tip of India.

Get Directions →

Gandhi Mandapam

Approx. 4.5 km • 15 min drive

A peaceful memorial near the seashore, built in memory of Mahatma Gandhi and his connection with Kanyakumari.

Get Directions →

Kanyakumari Beach & Sunrise Point

Approx. 5 km • 15–20 min drive

Experience the beautiful coastline and one of Kanyakumari's famous sunrise viewpoints.

Get Directions →

Vattakottai Fort

Approx. 10 km • 25–30 min drive

An impressive seaside fort with beautiful views of the coastline and surrounding landscape.
